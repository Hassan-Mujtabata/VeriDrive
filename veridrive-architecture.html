Authentication Flow
Email + password signup → Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA → 6-digit OTP email verification → Supabase session

report_history Table
Stores every completed verification report tied to user_id · full report_json (jsonb) · listing meta · composite_score · checked_at timestamp
uuid · user_id · listing_url · make · model · year · composite_score · report_json · checked_at

Users can only read and insert their own reports · enforced server-side via Supabase RLS policies · indexed on user_id + checked_at DESC
